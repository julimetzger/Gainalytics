# squat-person-2reps.mp4

Real person doing two barbell back squats (rear three-quarter view, 7.1 s, 1280x720, 30 fps).
Used by `video_import_playback.yaml` to prove the GH-75 hip/knee overlay: `squat-3reps.mp4` and
`squat-1rep.mp4` are synthetic (a plate on black), so the pose model finds no person in them.
